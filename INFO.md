# TARS — Project Information

## What is this project?

TARS is a friendly, voice-first helper created for rural cooperative communities in India. It is a physical presence you can walk up to and simply talk to — no typing, no forms, no smartphone needed.

It was built for places like PACS offices, Panchayat bhavans, and cooperative training centres, where people often need clear answers about rules, schemes, and money matters but may not read or write comfortably.

## Who is it for?

- Farmers and cooperative members
- Village communities visiting local cooperative offices
- People who prefer speaking in their own language over reading documents
- Anyone who needs guidance on cooperative-related matters and wants a patient, face-to-face explanation

## What can it help with?

TARS listens to your question and explains things in simple spoken words. You can ask about:

- Cooperative rules and by-laws in plain language
- Government schemes for cooperatives
- Crop insurance and financial basics
- Where to go and what to do if you have a complaint or grievance

If it does not know an answer for sure, it will say so and guide you toward a person who can help, rather than guessing.

## How do you use it?

You just walk up and speak. There is nothing to install, nothing to type, and nothing to learn beforehand. It talks back to you, shows subtitles for what it says, and treats voice as the main way to interact.

It is designed to be respectful, calm, and easy to approach — especially for elderly visitors and first-time users.

## Why does it exist?

Many important rules and support schemes are written in long, difficult documents that most people never get to read. TARS exists to bridge that gap: to bring that information out of files and into everyday conversation, in the language people actually speak.

The goal is simple — that a farmer can get a clear, trustworthy answer in the moment they need it, without depending on paperwork or middlemen.

## What this project is not

- It is not a phone chatbot or a mobile app you download.
- It is not a replacement for officers, lawyers, or cooperative staff.
- It does not make decisions for you — it explains, so you can decide with confidence.
