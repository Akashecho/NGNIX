Azure OpenAI hosts the reasoning model. TARS adds the session state, persona, retrieval, tool routing and streaming around it. Speech in arrives through Deepgram nova-3, with Azure Speech covering the languages nova-3 does not support and standing in if Deepgram is unavailable; replies are spoken by Azure Speech neural TTS and streamed back as audio.

Connecting…

Connection status is shown above the conversation. If it reads offline, start the backend and check the keys in .env, then reconnect.

Listening in a busy room

Speech is gated before it leaves your device. When you start the microphone, TARS measures the room for about a second, then sends only audio clearly above that noise floor — so a fan, a TV or a conversation nearby is not transcribed and cannot cut a reply short. The meter below moves with everything the microphone hears; only the part above the threshold is sent.

Leave that on unless you want to interrupt TARS mid-sentence. With it off, TARS can hear itself through your speakers and stop early — use headphones in that case.

With this on the microphone stays open and TARS only answers when you address it by name: “hey TARS, what is a PACS?”. Anything else you say is heard, ignored and never sent to the model. Turn it off for click-to-talk.
